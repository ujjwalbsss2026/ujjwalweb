MY NATIVE PLACE - BHOPAL

About Bhopal

Bhopal is the capital city of Madhya Pradesh. It is known as the City of Lakes. Bhopal is a beautiful and peaceful city. I like Bhopal because it has lakes, historical places and many interesting places to visit.

Upper Lake

Upper Lake is one of the most famous places in Bhopal. It is also called Bhojtal or Bada Talab. People visit the lake to enjoy the view and spend time with family and friends.

Taj-ul-Masajid

Taj-ul-Masajid is a famous historical mosque in Bhopal. It is one of the well-known landmarks of the city.

Why I Like Bhopal

  • It is my native place.
  • It has beautiful lakes.
  • There are many historical places.
  • It is an important city of Madhya Pradesh.
  • I am also studying in Bhopal.

My Favourite Thing About Bhopal

My favourite thing about Bhopal is its lakes and peaceful environment. The city has both traditional and modern areas, which makes it interesting.
